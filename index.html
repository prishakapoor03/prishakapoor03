About

I'm Prisha Kapoor, a psychology graduate from Christ (Deemed to be University) in Bangalore, now studying MSc People Analytics and Human Centric Management at UCL School of Management in London. I'm interested in how people behave at work, and in using data to understand it.

Before UCL, I spent over a year as an HR Analyst at Healthy Jeena Seekho, where I maintained and analysed HR and recruitment data and prepared reports on hiring and workforce processes. I also interned in HR at SML Isuzu Limited, supporting candidate assessment and writing job descriptions. My earlier clinical and research internships gave me a grounding in research methods.

Outside of work, I'm UCL's elected Academic Representative for my MSc cohort, and I have led student events and community projects. I'm looking for graduate opportunities across HR, including HR generalist, talent acquisition, people analytics, workforce planning, learning and development, HR operations and employee experience.
